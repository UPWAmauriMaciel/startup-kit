Step 1 of 3 · about 5 minutes

Unbox

Take the bike out of the box and check that everything is there.

1Part 1 of 4

Get a second person

An e-bike is heavier than a normal bike. Ask someone to help you lift it out of the box.

2Part 2 of 4

Open the box at the top

Open the flaps on the top of the box. That’s the easiest way to get the bike out.

3Part 3 of 4

Tip the box and slide the bike out

Lay the box flat on its side. Then slide the bike out together.

4Part 4 of 4

Check what’s in the box

Next to the bike you’ll find a start-up kit. We ticked each item on the box label. If an item is crossed out, your bike doesn’t need it.

Battery keysTo take the battery off and lock it back. Keep them safe.
Special toolSome bikes need it to open the battery. Keep it like a key.
Charger and cableOnly charge your battery with this charger.
Two pedalsMarked R and L. You’ll fit them in step 2.
ToolsAn Allen key and an open-end wrench.
DisplayOnly if it’s packed separately. It goes back on the handlebar.

Keep the box. You’ll need it if you ever send the bike back.

Something missing or damaged? Tell us right away, ideally with a photo.

Step 2 of 3 · about 10 minutes

Assemble

Your bike arrives almost ready. You only fit the pedals and straighten the handlebar, with the two tools from the box.

1Part 1 of 3

Fit the pedals

Each pedal has a letter on it: R for right, L for left, as you sit on the saddle. Both turn towards the front of the bike.

Screw each one in by hand first, then tighten with the open-end wrench.

Don’t force it. If it’s hard to turn, check the letter and the direction.

2Part 2 of 3

Straighten the handlebar

Loosen the bolt with the Allen key. Turn the handlebar so it’s straight with the front wheel. Then tighten until it doesn’t move.

Which one looks like your bike?

One bolt on top, sometimes under a rubber cap. Turn the one with the green arrow. Leave the red X alone.

Lift the lever to reach the bolt underneath. If your basket was taken off, straighten the handlebar first, then put the basket back.

Two bolts on the stem. Turn the ones with the green arrows. Leave the red X ones alone.

Sometimes we tilt it so the box closes. Then there’s an extra Allen key in the box for the bolts at the front.

Not sure? Send a photo
3Part 3 of 3

Fit the display

Only if your display came packed separately. Clip it back onto its holder on the handlebar.

It works a little differently on each model. Check the brand’s manual, or send us a photo.

Step 3 of 3 · about 5 minutes, plus charging

Charge & ride

Charge the battery, do five quick checks, and you’re ready.

1Part 1 of 2

Charge the battery

Charge it fully before your first ride. Do it indoors, on a dry surface, away from anything that burns easily.

1Unlock it with the key or special tool if you want to charge it off the bike.
2Use only the charger from the box.
3Put it back until it clicks. Lock it and take the key out before you ride.

Only one key? Take a photo of it. A locksmith can copy it from the number.

Tips for later↓
For later

Keep it running well

Read the manual

You’ll usually find it on the brand’s website.

Service it regularly

At a bike shop. More often if you ride every day.

Watch the wear parts

Brake pads, tyres and chain wear over time. Keep the chain clean.

Clean it gently

A damp cloth, never a pressure washer. Then oil the chain and keep it dry.

What we checked

  • Frame and fork checked for visible deformation, cracks and corrosion
  • Headset checked for play and adjusted if needed
  • Handlebar clamps checked and retightened if needed

  • Control units working properly
  • Fitting position and fastenings checked
  • Hub bearings checked for play
  • Spoke tension checked
  • Rim checked for wobble, damage and wear

  • Brake system, including levers, working and correctly fitted
  • Pads and discs checked for wear and a firm fit
  • Cables and hoses move freely and are undamaged
  • Hydraulic brakes checked for leaks and bite point
  • Roller brakes regreased, fit and function checked
  • Bottom bracket checked for play and a firm fit
  • Crank bolts and pedals checked and retightened if needed
  • Chainring checked for wear, chainring bolts retightened
  • Chainring carrier / lockring tight
  • Motor mounting bolts tight
  • Electrical connectors correctly seated
  • Speed sensor and spoke magnet correctly aligned
  • Lights working
  • Mountings and cable routing checked
  • Sidewall dynamo correctly positioned and fixed
  • Shifting checked and adjusted if needed
  • Chain and cassette checked for fit and wear, and lubricated
  • Derailleurs and gear cables checked, lubricated if needed
  • Shifters correctly and firmly fitted
  • Chain line checked
  • Saddle, seatpost and clamp checked for a firm, correct fit
  • Suspension and dropper seatposts working with the right amount of play
  • Suspension fork / shock function and pressure checked
  • Checked for external damage
  • Bearings checked for play
  • Mudguards and chain guard firmly fitted
  • Rack and luggage carriers checked for damage and a firm fit
  • Kickstand firmly fitted and working
  • Battery and mount sit firmly with no play
  • No visible external damage on the battery
  • Charger function checked
  • Software updated
